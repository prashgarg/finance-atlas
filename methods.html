Measurement notes

Methods

The site compares two descriptions of related finance ideas: what fund documents say products do, and what academic papers say about nearby concepts.

Method in brief

Short definitions for the current measurement object.

01

Product documents

Prospectus text is reduced to product descriptions: objective, exposure, instrument, benchmark, rule, horizon, and risk.

02

Academic neighborhoods

FrontierGraph provides finance concepts, relationships extracted from papers, and nearby papers around those concepts.

03

Link labels

We classify whether a product group and nearby papers share a strategy, mechanism, broader pattern, surface concept, or no useful link.
