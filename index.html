Method in brief

The site compares two graph objects: product-local graphs from fund documents and academic neighborhoods from the finance research graph.

01

Product documents

Prospectus text is reduced to product graphs: objective, exposure, instrument, benchmark, rule, horizon, and risk.

02

Academic neighborhoods

FrontierGraph provides the academic side: finance concepts, paper-local relationships, and graph neighborhoods around those concepts.

03

Bridge labels

We classify whether a product family and academic neighborhood share a strategy, mechanism, motif, surface concept, or no useful bridge.
