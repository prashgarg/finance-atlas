Method in brief

The site compares two descriptions of the same broad idea: what fund documents say the product does, and what academic papers say about nearby finance concepts.

01

Product documents

Prospectus text is reduced to simple product descriptions: objective, exposure, instrument, benchmark, rule, horizon, and risk.

02

Academic neighborhoods

FrontierGraph provides the academic side: finance concepts, relationships extracted from papers, and nearby papers around those concepts.

03

Bridge labels

We classify whether a product group and nearby papers share a strategy, mechanism, broader pattern, surface concept, or no useful link.
